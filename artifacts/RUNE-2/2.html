1 · In-game overlay — markers on the runes themselves

Your direction: mark each carry-over rune in place on the panel. RUNE-1 reports every gilded cell's exact rectangle, so a click-through layer over the capture region (same mechanism as the debug bounds overlay) draws a coloured frame on each one. Price text in the side column is unchanged. Example state: Power already carried; the unbound sprite in row 1 scores as "valuable" until you bind it.

Why frames and not fills: the game already colours the gilded frame gold and the glyphs by tier, so a semi-transparent tint would fight both. A 3px outer frame in a colour the panel never uses (green / orange / grey) plus a small badge stays readable at 45px cells. Frame thickness scales with the capture profile.

2 · Dashboard — Rune Library

Until bound it scores by its glyph colour (blue 0.5, otherwise 1) and never as gold.
